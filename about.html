Founder

A practitioner of product, people and transformation.

Balaji Subbaram is an R&D and engineering leader with more than 28 years of global experience across telecom, retail and healthcare. He has led end-to-end product lifecycles — from concept and new product development through sustenance — in highly regulated environments, with a consistent focus on quality, compliance and market readiness.

He has built and scaled cross-functional organisations spanning development, verification, PMO, clinical validation and technical publications at Motorola, Sasken, GE, Target, Philips and Baxter. His work includes global Agile (SAFe) transformations, multi-site programme delivery, digital product portfolio strategy, and coaching leaders and teams to deliver with clarity.

Manthana is his independent practice: a place to make that experience useful to students, professionals, delivery teams and enterprises who want capability, alignment and execution in one conversation.
